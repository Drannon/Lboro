\section*{Abstract}
The Woolwich Ferry has taken delivery of two diesel-electric ferries that have been beset with reliability issues. While they are now in service, they continue to be unavailable, with single boat operation being common. This presents an opportunity to provide an alternative vehicle to service the route. As a result, an initial architecture and requirements set for a battery-electric remotely operated unmanned vehicle (ROUV) has been developed. To ensure reliability and performance, along with meeting stakeholder expectations, this work lays out a verification and validation implementation using SysML1.5. Requirements have been validated, a plan for verification has been drafted, coverage has been examined, and preliminary verification has been performed to identify non-compliance early and to help explore the design space as it progresses.
